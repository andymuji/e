Key

  • Drop-off — fall riskThe floor falls away (a step down, a sunken area, stairs). Lined, downward triangle.
  • Trip hazard — robot can’t see itLow enough to trip on, and the lidar beam misses it. Hatched, triangle marker.
  • Robot could hit itNot a trip hazard, but the beam misses it. Cross-hatched, diamond marker.
  • Marginal — beam only grazes itThe robot may or may not see it. Dotted, half-filled square.
  • Robot should see itIt crosses the beam. Solid, round marker.
  • Unknown — treat as hazardIts heights are missing from the report. Dashed, question mark.
  • Unmeasured patchThe scan ran out or was hidden here. Check by eye.
  • WallNot counted as an obstacle.
  • Lidar beam heightThe robot’s lidar looks out flat at this height.

Heights come from a phone scan and are ±3 cm.
